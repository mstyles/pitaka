Pitaka

Search every book you own, and keep the passages that matter.

Pitaka is a desktop app for reading and researching your EPUB library. Import your books, search them all at once, and collect passages into bookmark folders.

Try it in your browser View on GitHub

The demo runs entirely in your browser with one built-in book, the Therīgāthā (Verses of the Senior Nuns). Try searching for patacara or craving.

Search across your whole library

Full-text search over every paragraph of every book, ranked by relevance, with the matching words highlighted. Search finds other forms of a word ("learn" also finds "learning"), or tick Exact words to match them exactly.

Case and diacritics are ignored, so samsara finds “saṃsāra” and patacara finds “Paṭācārā”. Quoted phrases, prefix* and AND/OR/NOT work too.

Read with the hit in context

Open a result and the reader jumps straight to that paragraph and highlights it, with the book's chapters alongside, so you can read on from where the search landed.

Keep passages in folders

Bookmark any paragraph into as many named folders as you like, one per topic or project, and come back to them later, each linked to its place in the book.

Get Pitaka

Pitaka is built with Tauri, Rust and SQLite. So far it has only been run on Linux, though Tauri also targets macOS and Windows. There are no prebuilt downloads yet, so for now it's built from source. The install steps are in the README.

It's early software: footnotes aren't handled specially yet, and the reader shows text without images or formatting. The known limitations are listed in full.

Pitaka is open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 licence. The demo's book is Verses of the Senior Nuns, translated by Bhikkhu Sujato and dedicated to the public domain (CC0) by SuttaCentral. Fonts: Fraunces, Literata and Source Sans 3, under the SIL Open Font License.
